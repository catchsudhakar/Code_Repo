About

Turning ambiguity
into paced execution

I am a Program Director with over 25 years of international experience helping banks execute large-scale Data, AI, and Digital Transformation programs. My career has been built at the intersection of strategy and delivery — translating complex mandates from Boards and Group Executives into structured programs with measurable impact.

At ANZ Bank, I led a $15M enterprise transformation spanning Data Engineering, AI Governance, Analytics, and Digital Assets — delivering $4M in annual benefits with a cross-functional team of 60+ across multiple regions. Prior to that, I built the technology organisation at Lygon, ANZ's blockchain fintech, and delivered $90M+ in cost optimisation as PMO Director.

I am widely recognised for creating clarity and cadence amid stakeholder complexity — coaching program teams, influencing executive sponsors at MD and GM level, and building the governance structures that give programs durability. My ANZ work on the Digital Assets program won the AFR's Most Innovative Companies award in 2025.

I hold an MBA, PMP, PRINCE2, and CSM, and am completing a Master of Teaching — a reflection of my belief that developing people is as important as delivering programs.

Thinking & Frameworks

How I Lead
Complex Programs

The Three Cs
Leadership Framework
My signature leadership philosophy for navigating ambiguity in large transformation programs. Where others see chaos, I apply structure — creating the conditions for teams to move fast and stakeholders to stay confident.

Clarity — Every program starts with making the mission, decisions, and priorities crystal clear across all levels. No ambiguity in direction.

Confidence — Building trust with executive sponsors and program teams through transparent risk management, consistent reporting, and honest communication.

Cadence — Establishing the rhythm that keeps multi-year programs moving: steering committee cycles, delivery sprints, benefit tracking, and team engagement.

ANZ AI Governance Framework
Enterprise AI Framework
Designed and implemented for a business unit of 1,000+ staff at ANZ Bank, this framework enabled responsible and accelerated AI delivery at enterprise scale.

The framework established reusable component architecture, a simplified governance model, and structured assessment criteria — enabling the review of 30+ AI use cases and the delivery of three AI initiatives to production, each with measurable productivity and customer experience outcomes.

One of the few governance frameworks in Australian banking that balanced risk rigour with speed-to-value.
